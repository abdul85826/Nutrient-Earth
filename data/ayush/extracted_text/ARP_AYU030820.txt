Background :  Background: Children with high IQ and memory have lower rates of poverty and dependency. Jyotishmati and Yastimadhu are both Medhya dravya to enhance IQ and memory describe in numerous Ayurveda classical texts. Aim &amp;objectives: To compare the efficacy of Jyotishmati with Yastimadhu on IQ and Memory in children with various Prakriti types. Material &amp; Methods: Total 60 healthy participants are enrolled and randomly divided into 2 equal main groups and 6 subgroups (VataPitta, VataKapha, PittaVata, PittaKapha, KaphaVata, KaphaPitta\ Intervention group\ Control group\ interventions were administered for 60 days and follow up for 60 days. Observations &amp; Result: While comparing Group J and Group Y, it showed highly significant at follow up in kapha-pitta groups with p&lt;0.001 in IQ and statistically significant with p&lt;0.05 in memory. Discussion: Kapha Pitta Prakriti dominant groups showed significant results in follow-up, because Kapha Prakriti persons are bestowed with Gunas (qualities\ slow grasping\ good retaining power\ good memory\ Medhavi (sharp grasping\ good retention power\ over other Prakriti Doshas. Conclusion: The prevalence of low IQ at pre-treatment in Group J was 3.32 % and Group Y was 6.7%. In memory, Group J was 16.7% and Group Y was 10% are on Average memory. Both the groups were 100% improve to Average IQ, and 100% improve to Good memory in Group J but 96.7% in Group Y at post-treatment and follow-up. Jyotishmati and Yastimadhu granules were significant in enhancing IQ and memory.
