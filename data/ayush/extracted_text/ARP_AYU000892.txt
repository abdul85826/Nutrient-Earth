Background :  Skin damage caused by the sun has become a serious concern. Many chemical sunscreens used for the purpose of avoiding the damage, penetrate deep into the skin causing photo allergic reactions as well as skin irritation. Plants have long been the focus of study concerning sun protection, leading to the development of many safer herbal sunscreen formulations. This paper evaluates the antioxidant and UV absorption property of the plant parts of Aloe vera, Camelia sinesis, Cinnamonium burmanii, Coffea arabica, Curcumin longa , Ocimum tenuiflorum, Prunus dulcis, , Santalum album, Syzygium aromaticum, Theobroma cacao and development of herbal formulations with the same. It was found that ethanolic extracts of seeds of Coffea arabica, flower buds of Syzygium aromaticum, bark of Cinnamomium burmanii and leaf of Ocimum tenuiflorum showed maximum antioxidant property and UV absorption activity. The Sun Protection Factor (SPF\ spectrophotometer was seen highest for the sunscreen cream made out of extracts of Cinnamomium burmanii.
