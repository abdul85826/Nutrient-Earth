Background :  Aim: Present study was designed to assess the safety levels of Centella asiatica (aerial parts\ albino rats. Materials &amp; Methods: Control group (I\ II, III and IV\ 500, 1,000 mg/kg. b.wt orally respectively for a period of 30 days. Changes in body weight were recorded at 10 days interval. On 32nd day blood samples were collected and the whole blood was used for hematological studies, DNA fragmentation assay and marker enyme levels were assessed in serum. The vital tissues (Liver, Kidney, Heart, Spleen, and Brain\ Trypan blue dye exclusion test\ fluorescence microscopy\ showed a significant increase in serum biomarkers (AST, ALT, BUN, Creatinine\ decrease in viability count in treatment groups in comparison to the control group. Histopathology also revealed a significant hepatic damage and a moderate degree of changes in the renal tissue. Conclusion: Based on the above observations it was concluded that the administration of Centella asiatica at 1,000 mg/kg b.wt for a period of 30 days may cause a significant damage to liver tissue in rats. Significance &amp; Impact of the study: This study signifies the organ specific toxicity of Centella asiatica.
