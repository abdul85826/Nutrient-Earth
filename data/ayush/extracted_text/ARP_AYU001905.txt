Background :  In Ayurveda, Tuttha (copper sulphate\ a group of minerals\ various diseases like skin diseases, eye disorders, obesity, pain, asthma, hyper acidity, hemorrhoids etc and the properties of Tuttha is mentioned as Cakshusya (good for eyes\ reduces itch\ reduces toxic effect\ antimicrobial\ present study was aimed to validate the Krimighna property of Tuttha bhasma by screening for antibacterial activity against two Gram-positive (Staphylococcus aureus, Bacillus subtilis\ Salmonella typhi and Escherichia coli\ two fungi (Candida albicans and Trichophyton rubrum\ Kirby-Bauer method. It was found that the antibacterial activity of Tuttha bhasma at 20 mg was equivalent to the inhibition shown by 1 mg of Gentamicin and antifungal activity was equivalent to standard drug Amphotericin B at 1 mg. The minimum inhibition concentration of the Tuttha bhasma was also estimated in the study to rule the susceptibility. The present study was found to be valid in its anti microbial activity or Krimighna as mentioned in Ayurvedic classics.
