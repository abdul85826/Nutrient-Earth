Background :  Agni has an important role to play in the physiological functioning of the body. It varies with the bodily constitution of individuals, season, age, and other factors. The uniqueness of each individual is determined by the Prakriti which deals with somatic and psychic development. The Prakriti directly impacts Jatharagni and determines the type of Jatharagni. A descriptive cross-sectional survey was conducted among healthy students from both genders aged between 18 and 30 years.
