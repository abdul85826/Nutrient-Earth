Background :  The present investigation was undertaken to screen preliminary phytochemical screening and antioxidant potential of two medicinally important plants of family Amaranthaceae. The crude powder of the plants gave different characteristic colors with different chemicals. The methanolic crude extracts of Digera muricata and Gomphrena celosioides were screened for their free radical scavenging properties by DPPH (1,1-diphenyl-2-picryl hydrazyl\ and roots of D. muricata.
