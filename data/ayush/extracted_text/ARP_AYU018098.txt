Background :  A comparative clinical trial on trophic ulcers in leprosy was undertaken to examine the effect of ayurvedic treatment on healing of trophic ulcers. There were 31 subjects in the study group and 31 in control group. The subjects were given ayurvedic treatment and the control subjects were treated conventional allopathic line treatment. The study proved that the ayurvedic treatment is a better alternative for treating trophic ulcers in leprosy.
