Background :  Diabetes mellitus is a long term disease with variable clinical manifestations, progression and number of complications but chorea as a presenting symptom in diabetes mellitus is very rare. In this paper an attempt has been made to highlight similar conditions explained in our classics. Tandavaroga explained by Sharangadhara has features similar to that of chorea. Hyperglycemia induced chorea can be understood as vataja prameha (Madhumeha\ involuntary movement disorders of new onset and emphasizes screening of diabetes mellitus in patients who develop sudden onset of chorea.
