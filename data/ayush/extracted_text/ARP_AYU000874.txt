Background :  Bovine Mastitis is an intra-mammary infection which is most common among the dairy cattle and continues to be the most costly disease to the dairy farmers. Presently, antibiotics are used for treatment of mastitis leading to the development of antibiotic resistant strains and consumer health problem. The ethno veterinary information about plants in Karnataka region to control Bovine mastitis was collected and the effect of different solvent extracts of Punica granatum L was investigated. Phytochemical analysis revealed the presence of bioactive compounds such as alkaloids, flavonoids, saponins, tannins, phenols, terpenoids etc. Each of the bioactive compounds were estimated and isolated separately by solvent-solvent extraction of Punica granatum. Saponins content was higher followed by flavonoids. All these bioactive compounds isolated from crude extracts were tested for antibacterial activity.
