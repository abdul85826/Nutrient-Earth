Background :  Objective: To evaluate effect of boiling for different durations on total phenolic content, total flavonoid content, antioxidant activity and alpha-amylase inhibitory activity of water extract of Lagenaria siceraria fruit, and to compare nutritional properties of its epicarp, mesocarp, seedsa pulp and pedicle. Methods: The bioactivities of aqueous extract of fruit was evaluated after boiling for 10, 20, and 30 min. Moisture, ash, fiber, protein, carbohydrate and lipid contents and vitamin C of epicarp, mesocarp, seeds pulp and pedicle were determined using standard methods.
