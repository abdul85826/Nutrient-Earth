Background :  Plants have been one of the major sources of medicines since the beginning of human civilization. Ipomoea sepiaria Koenig Ex. Roxb. is a medicinal plant considered as a source plant of the classical Ayurvedic drug Lakshmana. The root of this plant is known for the treatment of leucorrhoea and infertility. This study was undertaken to know antifungal effect of methanolic extract of root against the fungal strain Candida albicans which is responsible for leucorrhoea. The inhibitory effect was assessed by agar well diffusion method. The phytochemical profile was also carried out through primary phyto-chemical screening and HPTLC analysis. The result shows that the root of Ipomoea sepiaria is having phyto-constituents like carbohydrates, alkaloids, glycosides, flavonoids, phenolic compounds, tannin and saponin. The methanolic extract of root was found to possess significant antifungal activity and this supports the use of root in the treatment of leucorrhoea.
