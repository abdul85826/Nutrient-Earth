Background :  A study with an Ayurveda regimen consisting of Navaka guggulu, Sthaulyahara kashayam, a diet pattern and walk exercise for a treatment period of 3 mandalams (120 days\ medoroga were recruited for the study, and 34 patients formed the sample conforming to certain inclusion and exclusion criteria. Assessment of efficacy at the end of the 120-day treatment period vis-&#224;-vis baseline was done using a self-designed proforma considering the symptoms in accordance with the Ayurvedic system and the physical parameters chest (uras\ udara\ (sphik\ treatment regimen. At the end of the treatment period, noteworthy symptomatic improvement was found in the patients; statistically significant (p&lt;0.001\ normal ranges. No side effects were reported by the patients over the treatment period. The study is affirmative of the efficacy and safety of the Ayurvedic treatment regimen when administered for a period of 120 days to patients of medoroga. The importance of doing exercise in addition to intake of the medicines and a diet pattern in medoroga is emphasized.
