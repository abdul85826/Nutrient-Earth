Background :  VishaBilwadiGutika is a herbal formulation mentioned in KriyaKaumadhi used in snake bite cases in Kerala. In this study, an attempt has been made to explore the therapeutic value and mode of action in Wistar Albino Rats injected with cobra venom. The lyophilised snake venom of cobra was dissolved in saline and administered to rats. The lyophilised polyvalent anti snake venom was used as reference standard drug. The test drug VishaBilwadiGutika was evaluated for its efficacy to neutralise the cobra venom by assessing the lethality, haematological, biochemical and histological changes. The venom used in this study was found to have LD50 of 1098&#236;g/kg using OECD 425 protocol. Analysis of the result shows good protection by VishaBilwadiGutika (VBG\ protection was observed against high dose venom injection in acute conditions. Haematological examination of venom control group shows significant decrease in RBC and platelets in comparison to normal control group.
