Journal :  Journal of Pharmacy Research 
Year :  2011 
Volume :  4 
Issue :  10 
Page :  3455 - 3459 Abstract/ Summary/ Synopsis/ Outline : 
Background :  This study investigates the effect of the various formulations of metformin and Vernonia amygdalina extract loaded with PEGylated-mucin on haematological and liver indices in alloxan made diabetic rats. The effect on body weight, food and water consumption, organ weight, hematology and clinical biochemistry were studied. There were no significant differences in the body weight, organ weights and feeding habits between control and treated animals. Hematological analysis showed no marked differences in any of the parameters examined in either the control or treated groups. Chemical constituents show that anthracene glycosides, flavonoids, proteins, carbohydrates, reducing sugars, saponins and tannins were present. The liver enzymes showed no significant different from that of the control and the LD50 was of &gt; 650 mg/kg.
