Background :  Diabetic retinopathy is the most dreaded complication of ocular manifestation caused by diabetes. Newer conventional therapies report limited efficacy with considerable adverse effects. On contrary, experimental studies with herbal intervention have reported promising results. This study aims to evaluate the safety and efficacy of polyherbal formulation (coined as Mahagniwati\ standard oral treatment of antioxidants (Tab. Results : Among 66 patients, maximum patients had soft exudates, dot and blot haemorrhages and micro aneurysm.
