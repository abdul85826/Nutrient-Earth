Background :  Aim: The study was designed to investigate the chemopreventive potential of fl avonoidal fractions of Apium leptophyllum fruits (FFALF\ 25 µg in 0.1 ml acetone\ vehicle control\ (FFALF control, 5 mg/kg\ carcinogenic control, DMBA treated initially for 8 weeks\ (FFALF 5, 10 and 20 mg/kg respectively for 16 weeks along with DMBA treatment\ skin histopathology, and biochemical and antioxidant biomarkers were measured and compared with carcinogenic control as well as vehicle control.Results: The co-administration of FFALF with DMBA-treated groups showed significant (P = 0.001\ and normalized the status of lipid peroxidation with antioxidant biomarkers in a dose-dependent manner as compared to carcinogenic control.Conclusions: Thus, the present study suggests that the FFALF is non-carcinogenic and has chemopreventive potential on DMBA- induced carcinogenesis in mouse, which may be due to the modulation of cutaneous lipid peroxidation or enhancement of total antioxidant capacity.
