So according to pathogenesis and sign &amp; symptom, IBS can be a stage of Grahani Dosha. The present study was interventional, open label, prospective with time and single group. There was fifty two patients which clinically diagnosed case of IBS as per Rome III criteria were administered trial drugs Bilvadi Leha 10 gm twice daily orally after taking meal with lukewarm water as a Anupana within duration of 12 weeks.
