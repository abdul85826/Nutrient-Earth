Background :  Panchakarma therapy, the holistic treatment procedure of Ayurveda has been practiced from the ancient time in several clinical manifestations in geriatric including Pakshavadha (hemiplegia\ osteoarthritis\ paraplegia\ (neuromuscular disorders\ especially Panchakarma treatment as well as Keraleeya specialities have established its identity in global aspect as the most palatable approach for support and care in old age problems. In this present clinical study, 198 cases of Pakshavadha were studied at the In-patient department of Central Research Institute (Ay.\ days treatment schedule, whereas, study group I consisted with conventional Panchakarma therapy and group II and group III have been dealt with two separate Samana therapies. All study groups were assessed by on the basis of arbitrary neurological scoring as per their subjective and objective improvement. From this study, it has been seen that all study groups had significant improvement as per their treatment regimen. Moreover, as per global assessment and feeling of well being of the patients, it may be concluded that study group I with conventional Panchakarma procedures and Rasausadhies along with Keraleeya specialties in group III were more effective when compared with other group. This study may be treated as the pioneer approach for evaluation of effectiveness of Ayurvedic treatment along with Panchakarma procedure with modern scientific outlook.
