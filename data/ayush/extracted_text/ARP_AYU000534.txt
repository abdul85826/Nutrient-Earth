Background :  The present study aimed at evaluating the Nutritional profile, Antimicrobial activity and Phytochemical Screening of Wild edible fruit of Ziziphus jujuba. The fruits have been found to rich in nutrients such as crude protein, carbohydrates, crude fiber, ash content (4.26, 36.31, 6.12, 2.58 %\ 1.17, 0.87, 2.98 and 0.73 mg/gm\ ethanolic fruit extracts of Ziziphus jujuba showed significant activity 16&#177;1mm, 15&#177;1mm and 15&#177;1mm against Escherichia coli (MTCC 729\ MTCC 1925\ MTCC 443\ screening for the presence of glycosides, flavonoids, phenols, resin and tannins. However, alkaloids were absent. This analysis revealed that, the fruits contained higher value of fat, protein, fiber and minerals as compared to the cultivated fruits with apple and 200 gm fruits contain sufficient amount of nutrients, required per day by a person. Consumption of fruits may promote general health and well-being as well as reduce the risk of chronic diseases.These findings confirms that the Zizyphus jujuba may be potentional source for the formulation of nutraceuticals or natural foods.
