Background :  The inflammatory markers like C-reactive protein (CRP\ IL-6\ TNF-a\ be determinants of risk for type I Diabetes Mellitus (DM\ adipokines that could contribute to vascular inflammation and atherosclerosis. CRP and IL-6 are two important sensitive biomarkers associated with hyperglycaemia, insulin resistance and type II diabetes mellitus and altered levels are responsible for development of CHD. TNF-a also is a possible mediator of insulin resistance in obesity and diabetes. Modulation of endothelial function by reducing inflammatory process may be important in diabetes to prevent atherosclerotic complications.Keeping the above facts in view a clinical trial was conducted involving 60 diagnosed type-II Diabetes Mellitus cases of both sexes with age range of 40-65 years showing elevated CRP-IL-6, TNF-a with high BMI. 30 cases were assigned to receive hydro-alcoholic extract of Salacia reticulata (900 mg/day in two divided doses\ Glynase and 30 cases were kept on Glynase alone for six months to compare the results. At the end of six months the elevated pro- inflammatory biomarkers CRP, IL-6, TNF-a including BMI reduced significantly in type-II diabetes subjects suggesting modified endothelial dysfunction and reduced CHD risk. it is proposed that since the management of diabetes is a lifelong process, the prevention and management of associated inflammatory markers is essential to prevent the adverse cardiac event. The present test drug is a safer choice of remedial measure for this purpose.
