Background :  Background: Chronic suppurative otitis media (C.S.O.M.\ initial episode of acute otitis media and characterized by the recurrent discharge from the middle ear through a tympanic perforation. In Ayurveda, C.S.O.M. can be correlated with Karnasrava. According to Acharya Sushruta, Karnasrava is &quot;discharge of pus from a Vata affected ear which may result from a head injury or due to immersion in water or else due to suppuration or bursting of an abscess.&quot; In modern science, most of the approaches for the treatment of C.S.O.M. have been surgical, which are often expensive, complicated, and do not provide satisfactory hearing improvement; also these are inaccessible in many developing countries. Considering all these points, there is a need to search for an effective, preventive, and safe treatment.Objective: The objective of this study is to evaluate the efficacy of Karnapichu and Karnadhupana along with Rasnadi Guggulu on Karnasrava. Results: Both the groups showed highly significant results in the subjective parameters and nonsignificant results in the objective parameters of Karnasrava.
