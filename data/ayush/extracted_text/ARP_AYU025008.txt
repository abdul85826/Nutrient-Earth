Background :  Background: Personalized approach in prescribing health advocacies and treatment of diseases based on Prakriti vis-a-vis human constitution is unique to Ayurveda. Standardization in the assessment of Prakriti is the need of the hour. The methodology for designing the Prakriti assessment scale (PAS\ standardized PAS encompassing its validation and reliability testing.Aim: The aim of this study was to develop and validate an assessment scale to assess Ayurvedic Prakriti in accordance with the standardized methodology of questionnaire development.Material and methods: The study was conducted on a designed PAS for its validation in terms of face, content, construct, and criterion validity. The reliability testing of the validated PAS has been evaluated through intra-rater and inter-rater reliability testing estimated using the Kappa statistic.Results: The standardized PAS consisting of 91 predictors grouped into 30 domains in four traits (physical, physiological, psychological, and behavioral\ been developed.
