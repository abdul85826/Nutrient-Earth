Background :  Background: Diabetes mellitus is a group of metabolic alterations characterized by hyperglycemia resulting from defects in insulin secretion, action or both. The present study was to identify the antidiabetic activity of ethanolic (EESP\ AESP\ Strychnos potatorum seeds against alloxan induced diabetes in rats. Results: The LD50 the extracts were found to be 2000 mg/kg b.w., p.o., The EESP and AESP extracts exhibited significant hypoglycemic and significant reductions in the biochemical parameters (Total cholesterol, Triglycerides, LDL and VLDL\ showed significant activity comparing with the standard glibenclamide, but activity of the extracts were found to be similar or slightly decreased as compared to the standard. The histopathology results confirmed the finds. These results showed the medicinal significance of the plant. Conclusion: The present study concluded that the Strychnos potatorum linn proved useful in the treatment of diabetes.
