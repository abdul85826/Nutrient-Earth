Background :  The present study was carried out to evaluate the antibacterial activity of different extracts of Mucuna pruriens L. against Escherichia coli and Staphylococcus aureus. Three extracts such as aqueous extract, ethanol extract and chloroform were prepared for the present study. Agar well diffusion method was used for evaluating antibacterial activity against the above said pathogens. Results suggested that aqueous extract of Mucuna pruriens showed more antibacterial activity when compared to ethanol extract, chloroform extract &amp; norfloxacin against E. coli and S. aureus.
