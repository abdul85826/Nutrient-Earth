Background :  The presence of natural antioxidants in plants is well known. They are gaining a lot of importance due to their associated health benefits. In the present study methanolic and petroleum ether extracts of leaves of Tecoma stans, Heliotropium indicum and Mimosa pudica which are used as traditional medicinal plants were evaluated for their antioxidant and total phenolic content. Antioxidant activity was determined by ferric thiocyanate (FTC\ TBA\ Phosphomolybdenum method\ was also determined. FTC, TBA and total antioxidant activity for petroleum ether and methanol extracts, Heliotropium indicum exhibited a higher antioxidant activity. Total phenolic content was estimated for methanolic extracts and expressed in terms of &#181;g gallic acid equivalents/mg dry weight. The total phenolic content estimated for methanolic extract was in the order of Heliotropium indicum &gt; Tecoma stans &gt; Mimosa pudica. The results obtained indicate the leaves of all the three plants are a potential source of antioxidants.
