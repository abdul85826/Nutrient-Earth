Background :  The present study was carried out to evaluate the hypoglycemic activity and antihyperglycemic activity of aqueous extracts of whole of Vinca rosea in normal and in alloxan induced diabetic rats. The aqueous extract of Vinca rosea (30mg/Kg, 100mg/Kg, 300mg/Kg bd.wt\ and the dose(100mg/Kg\ 30-40%\ collected at desired intervals of time and analyzed for blood glucose by GOD/POD method. Gliclazide, a second generation sulfonylurea was selected for the interaction study with that of the Vinca rosea. The doses of gliclazide (1mg/Kg, 2mg/Kg and 4mg/Kg bd. wt\ normal and diabetic rats and the dose (2mg/Kg bd. wt\ administration of Vinca rosea significantly increased the hypoglycemic and antihyperglycemic activity of gliclazide.
