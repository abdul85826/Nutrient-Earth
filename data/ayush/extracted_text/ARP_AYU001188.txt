Background :  The principal focus of Ayurveda is on maintaining good health and adopting a healthy way of life. Concept of prakriti as conceived by Ayurveda is one of the unique jewels of Ayurveda that glorify this stream of medicine. According to this concept in society Vata Pradhan Prakriti persons are always having Alpabala. Aarogya (good health\ to take care of such alpa bala Vata Prakriti persons and they should be treated with Bruhan procedures. Matra basti is one of the best procedures for Balavardhan as it is free from any complications, acts on Vata sthana and foremost property of Tila Taila used in Matra Basti is its Balavardhan karma. Hence to study about the concept of Prakriti, Bala and Basti, moreover to evaluate effect of Matra Basti on Sharirik Bala of Vata Prakriti persons, this study has been carried out. Ayurvedic Parameters such as Ardha shakti Vyavam lakshanas, i.e. Mukha- Shwas, Talu-Shosha, Kapal-Sveda and time for exhaustion; and modern parameters like Fatigue Index and Harward step test(HST\ used to evaluate the effect of Matra Basti. With the help of HST, Fatigue Index was calculated before and after therapy of Matra Basti. The results were statistically evaluated. It is revealed that Matra Basti has promising results with respect to Balavardhan of Vata Prakriti persons.
