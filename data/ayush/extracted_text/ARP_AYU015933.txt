Background :  Amlapitta is one of the most common diseases seen in society. The word Amlapitta is made up of Amla and Pitta. Aacharya Kashyapa was the first person who described Amlapitta as a separate disease. Madhav Nidan, Bhavprakash, and Yogratnakara have also described it very well.The disease in which the sour quality of Pitta dosha aggravates and changes to extreme acidic sourness is called Amlapitta.
