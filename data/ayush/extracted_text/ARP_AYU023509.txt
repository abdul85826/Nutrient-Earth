Background :  Aim: To examine the efficacy and safety of the toothpaste containing Rhizoma Chuanxiong and Rhizoma Imperatae extracts in reducing gingivitis. Method: A double-blind clinical trial was conducted, in which 120 volunteers were randomly assigned to the test group (N = 60\ N = 60\ Rhizoma Chuanxiong and Rhizoma Imperatae contains the main components of cylindrin, carotene, 5-hydroxytryptamine, potassium, and calcium. The control group used placebo toothpaste containing neither Rhizoma Chuanxiong extract nor Rhizoma Imperatae extract. Plaque, gingivitis, and bleeding were assessed at the baseline, prior to the supragingival scaling, and at 4, 8, and 12 weeks. Results: During the trial, both test and control groups showed a decreasing trend compared to the baseline. At the end of 12 weeks, with respect to Gingival Index (GI\ Bleeding Index (BI\ BOP%\ (GI, P&lt;0.001, BI, P&lt;0.001, and BOP%, P&lt;0.001, resp.\ GI, BI, and BOP% scores between the two groups. However, the decrease became statistically significant at next two intervals (GI, P&lt;0.001, BI, P&lt;0.001, and BOP%, P&lt;0.001, resp.\ were no treatment-related adverse events reported. Conclusion: The toothpaste containing Rhizoma Chuanxiong and Rhizoma Imperatae extracts was well tolerated and significantly reduced gingivitis and bleeding after usage for 12 weeks. There was better improvement at molars, and the more serious the baseline status was, the better the efficacy was.
