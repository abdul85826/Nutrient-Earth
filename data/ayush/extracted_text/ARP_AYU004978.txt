Background :  OBJECTIVE: To study the effect of fine powder of ginger on lipid level in volunteer patients.METHODS: This is a double blind controlled clinical trial study in 2 cardiac clinics Cardiac Disease Clinic, Babol, north of Iran, between April to May 2004. We randomly divided the patients with hyperlipidemia into 2 groups, treatment group (receiving ginger capsules 3 g/day in 3 divided doses\ (lactose capsule 3 g/day in 3 divided doses\ drinking, pregnancy and peptic ulcer were excluded. Lipid concentrations profile before and after treatment was measured by enzymatic assay.RESULTS: Forty-five patients in the treatment group and 40 patients in placebo group participated in this study. There was a significant reduce in triglyceride, cholesterol, low density lipoprotein (LDL\ VLDL\ in each group (p&lt;0.05\ (p&lt;0.05\ VLDL level of placebo was higher than ginger (p&gt;0.05\ compared to placebo.
