Background :  The term miscarriage is used to describe a pregnancy that fails to progress, resulting in death and expulsion of the embryo. When this happens more than once, it is termed as recurrent pregnancy loss. Sushrutacharya in his Samhita has described this condition and has also suggested methods to prevent it.Charaka Acharya says that if appropriate care is taken during early pregnancy, along with proper drugs, healthy diet and good exercise, then the woman gives rise to a healthy child. The principle aim of this trial is to evaluate the importance of administration of selected drugs and their combination during the pregnancy to prevent the miscarriage and avoid pregnancy loss.A liquid herbal formulation mentioned by Sushrutacharya was prepared in cows milk, boiled, cooled to room temperature and administered to pregnant women orally at different stages of pregnancy. It was observed that in comparison to modern treatment this therapy had much better results and satisfactory clinical output.
