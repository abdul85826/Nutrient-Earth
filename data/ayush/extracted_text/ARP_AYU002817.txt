Background :  Type 2 diabetes can be correlated to apathyanimittaja prameha or avarana janya madhumeha. Here insulin antagonists are not allowing it to metabolize blood glucose. This paper tries to evaluate the effect of Saptarangyadi Ghanavati in apathyanimittaja Prameha. Saptarangyadi Ghanavati has been administered to six patients with type 2 diabetes to evaluate its role in FBS, PPBS, and serum insulin levels. Most of the ingredients of the formulation are pramehahara and rasayana. The results show statistically significant (paired t test\ postprandial serum insulin level along with statistically significant decrease in the BMI and serum cholesterol level and statistically insignificant decrease in FBS and PPBS levels. It ensures good glycemic control in these cases in which sulphonylurea group of oral anti-hyperglycemic agents is not much of use. It can also reduce the dosage of exogenous insulin or oral anti-diabetic synthetic drugs, thereby preventing higher cost and adverse effects associated with use of these modern drugs.
