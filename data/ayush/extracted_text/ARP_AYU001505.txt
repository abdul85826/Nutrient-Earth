Background :  Arbudaharana rasayana is an anubhuta yoga (Self experienced compound medicine\ formulation, practicing by traditional healers of Orissa as a folklore medicine, for the management of Arbuda. The formulation was studied clinically in 10 patients of non operable malignancy of different regions of body like scalp, vagina, oesophagus, bladder and oral cavity. The drug was given in powder form, 5 gm twice a day, orally with Luke warm water for two months. Symptomatic relief was observed in treated patients with overall improvement in quality of life. The drugs might have been effective due to anti-cancerous, antioxidant and immuno-modulator activities. During follow up period there was no any adverse effect of drug observed.
