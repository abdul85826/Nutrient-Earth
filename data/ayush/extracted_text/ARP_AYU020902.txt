Background :  Malaria is a widespread tropical disorder characterized by relapsing febrile illness, chills, rigors, headache etc. Controlling / eradication of the disease is difficult to achieve and recurrence is the commonest phenomenon in endemic areas. Around 1.1 million deaths are due to Malaria worldwide. Malaria can be compared with Vishamajwara based on the irregular onset and remission. Present study is the prophylactic trial of Malaria. Study carried out with a poly-herbal formulation called Indukanthaghritam on 67 patients, of which, 35 (74.46%\ showed Good response, 11 (23.40%\ 2.12%\ lip, 09 patients dropped out from the study, and 11 patients were LAMA. To create evidence based therapy, assessment of serum immunoglobulins done before and after treatment.
