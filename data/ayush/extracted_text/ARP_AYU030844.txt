Background :  Introduction: Dyslipidemia is the disorder of lipoprotein metabolism manifested by an elevation of the total cholesterol, the low- density lipoprotein (LDL\ HDL\ concentration in the blood. Todays unhealthy food habits and sedentary lifestyle are the main causative factors for Dyslipidemia. It is not defined in Ayurveda, it can be related to a number of conditions, including &quot;Medoroga, Rasa Raktagata Snehavruddhi Shonitabhishyandana, and Dhamanipratichaya&quot; based on similarities in etiology, pathogenesis, and manifestations. Aim: Comparative evaluation of Efficacy of Kulattha Gutika and Atorvastatin in the Management of Dyslipidemia (Medoroga\ comprises total 60 patients of Dyslipidemia randomly divided into two equal groups. Patients were assessed for Objective parameters like Lipid profile (S. TCH, TG, LDL, HDL, VLDL\ done on 0 and 45th day. Result: Statistically significant improvement was observed in objective parameters like TCH, HDL, LDL, VLDL, and TG in both groups.
