Background :  The main aim of the present study was to evaluate the anti-inflammatory properties of Ayurvedic preparation of Haritaki (Terminalia Chebula\ vehicle\ buttermilk, Guda Haritaki powder with distilled water and Guda Haritaki powder with anupana (vehicle\ was evaluated against the standard drug indomethacin at a dose of 10 mg/kg orally. Wistar rats of either sex of five numbers in each group was undertaken for study and evaluated by carrageenan-induced paw edema method. Haritaki powder with Anupana (vehicle\ Haritaki powder with distilled water treated groups showed significant reduction in the carrageenan induced paw edema (P&lt; 0.01\ compared to control group rats. The Ayurvedic preparations of Haritaki have potential anti-inflammatory activity and hence could be established by further more studies.
