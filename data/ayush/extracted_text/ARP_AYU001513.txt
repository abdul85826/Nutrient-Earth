Background :  Epilepsy is considered as the most dreadful disease &amp; its prevalence has been estimated at 510 person per 1000.The present study was designed to investigate the antiepileptic potential of Coleus amboinicus Lour. on Maximal electroshock (MES\ seizure model in rats. Total 50 Albino rats of wistar strain were used in the study. Animals were divided into 5 groups each having 10 rats. Fresh juice of Parnayavani i.e. Coleus amboinicus (2 ml/rat\ Parnayavani (500 mg/bw p.o.\ 25 mg/kg bw i.p.\ water as vehicle. In MES model delay in HLE was taken as end point. C. amboinicus leaf juice (CALJ\ CAalE\ having highest efficacy as antiepileptic drug in comparison to its aq. extract. in MES model . Thus we observed that leaf juice as well as alcoholic extracts of Coleus amboinicus possess anticonvulsant and neuroprotective activity.
