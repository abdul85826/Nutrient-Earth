Background :  In modern medicine, metabolic disorders contribute many diseases out of which malnutrition is a major health problem in todays era. In Ayurveda, such condition like malnutrition is described as Kuposhanjanya Vyadhi and Balshosha term is used for it in Vishwakosha (part. 11\ the preventive and curative aspect of malnutrition by means of Aahariya and Aushadhiya dravya Ashwagandha in the palatable form, i.e. granules with anupan milk as suggested by Acharya Vagbhata. The aim of this paper is to study the efficiency of sadugdha Ashwagandha granules in Balashosha. In the present study, 60 patients of Balshosha in the age group 25 years were selected and randomly classified into two groups, i.e. experimental and control group. In the experimental group, chemically analysed Ashwagandha in granules form and in the control group Pakwa Ardra kharjur with anupan dugdha (up to 200 ml\ malnutrition (Grades I and II\ on subjective criteria, i.e. sign and symptoms of Balashosha mentioned in Ayurvedic text graded as 0 to 4 scale, and objective parameters, i.e. Quetlet index, weight, mid arm and chest circumference, etc., were clinically evaluated. Patients were keenly observed and assessed before and after treatment, and collected data were analysed statistically. The effect of therapy shows effective and encouraging results in both groups.
