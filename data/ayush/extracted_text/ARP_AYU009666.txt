Background :  Background: High regenerative and proliferative capacity of blood and its components renders it to be at higher risk of adverse drug reactions (ADRs\ several metabolic disorders. Objective: It is prudent to come up with some therapeutic entity that can prevent this damage and protects the blood from these ADRs. Materials and Methods: We examined protective effects of Plumbago zeylanica (PZ\ plumbagin (PL\ SD\ Phz\ Hgb\ (RBCs\ MCH\ MCHC\ oxidant enzymes superoxide dismutases 2 and 3 (SODs\ Nfer-1 and 2\ using quantitative real-time polymerase chain reaction (PCR\ terms of deviated endpoints of blood indices. In PLtreated groups protective response was obtained in terms of normal endpoints of blood indices. In PCR studies, we observed the similar trend. Thus, it can be postulated that PL exerts its protective effects via modulation of anti- oxidant enzymes. Conclusion: The study proves that PL can be employed against combatting the ADRs associated with several therapeutic treatment regimens. Similar studies employing such pharmacological entities and their combinations may further prove to be effective against ADRs, especially in the context of blood cells.
