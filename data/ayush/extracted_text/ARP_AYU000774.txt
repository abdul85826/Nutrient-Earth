Journal :  Journal of Pharmacy Research 
Year :  2009 
Volume :  2 
Issue :  3 
Page :  563 - 568 Abstract/ Summary/ Synopsis/ Outline : 
Background :  The present study evaluated the effect of three Ayurvedic formulations and five medicinal plants commonly used in the treatment of diabetes on a-amylase, a-glucosidase, sucrase and glucose uptake by yeast cells in vitro. All the Ayurvedic formulations inhibited the enzymes to varying degrees. Although all the medicinal plants studied inhibited a-amylase to an extent of 16-74%, only Eugenia jambulana and Ficus racemosa inhibited all the three enzymes while, Tinospora cardifolia and Gymnema sylvestrae activated a-glucosidase and sucrase. Aegle marmelos inhibited the activity of a-glucosidase. All the samples except Aegle marmelos significantly increased (p=0.01\ in yeast cells. The enhancement of glucose uptake was dependent on both the sample and glucose concentration. It is proposed that, the mechanism of hypoglycemic action varies greatly from one medicinal plant to other and hence, there is a need for in depth research to establish the mode of action of each medicinal plant for their effective utilization therapeutic agents.
