Background :  A Clinical trial of Indigenous compound drug Nishamalaki was carried out inthe CRI (Ayurveda\ Madhumeha. For this study, patients wererandomly divided into two groups of 25 individuals each and they were termed as group A andB. The individuals of group a were administered Nishamalaki in a dose of one gram twice dailyalong with diet control, the patients were followed every fort nightly. A significant improvementin the symptoms along with lowering of blood glucose level was observed in the individuals ofgroup B, while the individuals of group A responded initially but could not sustain the same.
