Background :  Most plants have magico-religious and socio-cultural significance. The use of plant materials for the treatment of various diseases is very common in third world countries. Herbal medicines as prescribed by the rural people have not been completely scientifically validated. Azima tetracantha Lam. is one such plant used in herbal medicine practices in the villages of South Kerala. The root, root bark and leaves mainly find therapeutic uses. The decoction prepared from these plant parts, along with other ingredients is given to treat diarrohea. The leaves find many uses among the tribal people. One such practice among tribal people is to administer Azima tetracantha leaves crushed with neem leaves, to puerperal women immediately after confinement. It is given with food as a remedy for rheumatism and its juice is used to relieve cough. The leaf is used as a snake bite remedy and as an expectorant. An infusion of leaves is used to treat venereal diseases. In this context, there arises a need to study the proximate and nutritional constituents in the leaves of Azima tetracantha, so as to validate its medicinal properties. Quantitative analysis, mineral profile, proximate composition and anti nutritional factors were analyzed as part of the study. Total amount of carbohydrate, proteins, lipids, ascorbic acid, and tocopherol were quantitatively determined. Potassium (17200 ppm\ highest in the leaves. Moisture content, dry weight, bulk density, total ash content, acid soluble and insoluble ash content were determined for the proximate analysis.
