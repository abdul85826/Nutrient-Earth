Materials and Methods: In this 12-week, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, multi-centric study, T2DM patients which inadequate glycemic control with background stable dose medication of sulfonylurea was screened (n = 120\ therapy of IDM1 and placebo, 700 mg three times daily for 12 weeks.
