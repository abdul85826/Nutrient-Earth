Background :  Pelvic Inflammatory Disease (PID\ tenderness, abnormal vaginal discharge, chills and fever. Various yonivyapats, presenting with the above said symptoms like adhodarasula, sola in maithuna, jvara and yonisrava include vatala, sannipatika, paripluta, mahayoni and so on. This article reviews the clinical effect of an Ayurvedic formulation Sivagutika along with balaniruhavasti on certain symptoms of PID through clinical trials with an intention to maximize its clinical effectiveness and hasten its integration into wider clinical practice. Sivagutika was administered twice daily with honey after food for sixty days. Yogavasti, i.e 8 vasti were administered to each patient - 3 anuvasanavasti and 3 asthapanavasti were administered on alternate days and 2 anuvasanavasti were administered at the end. In most of the cases, there was progressive reduction in the symptoms with time, indicating the efficacy of the therapy in PID.
