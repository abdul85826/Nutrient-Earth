Aims: The study aimed to investigate the antiobesity effects of Cuminum cyminum L. distillates (CD\ groups: normal diet (ND\ HFD\ Results: The CDtreated group significantly (p &lt; 0.001\ insulin, glucose, leptin and pancreatic lipase activity in HFD-induced obese rats.
