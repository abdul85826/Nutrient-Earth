Journal :  Journal of Drug Research in Ayurvedic Sciences 
Year :  2018 
Volume :  3 
Issue :  1 
Page :  15 - 22 Abstract/ Summary/ Synopsis/ Outline : 
Background :  Aim: Cardiospermum halicacabum L. is a climbing plant under Sapindaceae family. The plant leaf is used by traditional healers for treatment of various illnesses. Considering the ethno-medicinal value of C. halicacabum, the present study was taken up to evaluate single- dose acute toxicity study and repeated dose 28-day oral toxicity study (subacute\ hydroalcoholic extract of C. halicacabum leaf was administered orally to Wistar rats as single dose (2000 mg/kg bwt\ for continuous 28 days in repeated dose subacute toxicity study at three dose levels (250, 500, and 1000 mg/kg bwt\ as per Organisation for Economic Cooperation and Development (OECD\ observed during the study period. Results: The study showed that there were no mortality and morbidity in the test groups. The detailed biochemical and hematological investigations evaluated in control and test groups evidenced the safety of the extract as there were no significant differences among control and test groups. Histopathology study in vital organs of test groups showed no significant abnormalities. Conclusion: The study concluded that the hydroalcoholic extract of C. halicacabum was safe at the prescribed dosages in Wistar albino rats.
