Background :  Tectaria coadunata (Wall. Ex Hook &amp; Grev.\ Kukkutnakhi\ obesity. Also it is used in vegetable curry or salad. Till date, a detailed pharmacognostical study on its rhizome is not available. Hence, the present study was carried out to document, morphological, microscopical and histochemical features of the rhizome, following standard procedures. Macroscopic studies showed the rhizome of 1020 cm in length and 3 cm in diameter, short creeping, up to 3 cm thick, densely scaly at the apex. Rhizome showed the presence of cuticle, parenchyma, meristele and vascular bundles. Powder microscopy of the dried rhizomes showed epidermal cells with tannin, starch grains, fragments of ramenta, and prismatic crystals.
