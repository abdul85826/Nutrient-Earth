Background :  The anticataract activity of the Ervatamia coronaria leaf extract was evaluated against naphthalene induced cataract in Wistar albino rats. Thirty rats in five groups were used for the study. Ervatamia coronaria leaf extract at dose levels (200 and 400 mg/kg\ and Vitamin E (50 mg/kg\ simultaneously with naphthalenefor 25 days. Naphthalene (0.5 g/kg for first 3 days and 1 g/kg thereafter for a period of 25 days\ induce cataract. The percentage of cataract incidence and opacity index were examined using ophthalmoscope. Naphthalene produced a marked mature cataract and an increase in the opacity index at various stages. The extract treated animals showed decrease in the onset and maturation of cataract against naphthalene challenge. From the results it was concluded that Ervatamia coronaria leaf extract protected the cataract maturation induced by naphthalene and it exhibited anticataract activity.
